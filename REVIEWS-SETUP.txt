- Dates are relative labels exactly as shown in the screenshots; no calendar date is inferred. Ratings show the five-star row visible at capture time — not a verified live rating. Product titles and prices visible in screenshots are historical, not current offers.

REVIEW CONTROLS
- Indicator buttons have 44 x 44 CSS-pixel hit areas with small visual dots; 24 dots, one per slide.
- Pause reviews sets a persistent pause. Resume reviews enables rotation again only when interaction pauses are clear.
- Focus anywhere in the carousel pauses movement. Hover/touch over review content pauses it temporarily. Background tabs pause playback.
- Reduced motion disables autoplay and animated transitions. Returning to normal motion leaves playback paused until Resume reviews is selected.
- Manual selection remains available in reduced motion. Slides expose position/attribution ("Review N of 24 from @handle"); only the selected slide (and its source link) is exposed to keyboard/assistive technology; the rest stay inert.
- Automatic changes do not announce full reviews. Manual selection announces the review position and author.

CONTENT SAFETY
Do not invent reviews, totals, ratings, URLs, review dates, customer consent, or platform verification. Local screenshot transcription is not live-profile verification. The "24" count label must stay tied to the locally evidenced entries only and must not be presented as the live Carousell total. Customer feedback is not a Carousell endorsement.
